## A-11 Field

<img src="a11-field/img/icon.png" alt="A-11 Field icon" width="96" align="left" style="margin-right:16px;border-radius:18px;">

A watch face for Wear OS in the style of the **A-11**, the WWII-era U.S.
military field watch — built to a government specification rather than one
brand's design, and often called "the watch that won the war." It brings that
clean, legible, no-nonsense dial to your wrist: a high-contrast face, bold
Arabic numerals, a full minute track with numbered five-minute markers, a center
**sweep seconds** hand, and slim tapering "alpha" hands.

<br clear="left">

![The six looks of A-11 Field](a11-field/img/looks.png)

Customize it from the watch face editor:

- **Dial style** — Black, Black railroad (twin concentric "railroad track" minute
  ring), or White.
- **Condition** — New issue (crisp and factory-fresh) or Antique patina (a warmed
  dial with aged-tan lume, subtle foxing, and mottling).

Six looks in all, from a pristine new field watch to a well-worn vintage piece.
No ads, no trackers, and no permissions — it simply shows the time, and it is
built with the Watch Face Format for efficient, battery-friendly performance.

## About
