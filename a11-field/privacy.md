# Privacy Policy — A-11 Field

_Last updated: 18 September 2026_

A-11 Field is a Wear OS watch face published by idlewait.

## Data collection

A-11 Field does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The watch face is built entirely with the Watch Face Format (WFF) — it contains
no application code, no network access, no analytics, and no advertising. It
displays the time and the user-selected dial style locally on the watch and
nothing else. It does not read your location, health/sensor data, contacts, or
any other personal information.

## Permissions

A-11 Field requests no runtime permissions.

## Third parties

A-11 Field does not include any third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising
libraries. No data is sent to any third party because no data is collected.

## Children

Because the watch face collects no data at all, it is suitable for users of all
ages.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a
revised "Last updated" date.
